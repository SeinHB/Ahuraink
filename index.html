OUR STORY

Born From Darkness,
Built on Craft.

Dark Ink Studio was founded in 2015 in the heart of Istanbul by a collective of artists obsessed with the darker side of beauty. We believe a tattoo is not just ink — it is a permanent story, a ritual, a declaration.

Every piece we create is custom-designed. No flash sheets, no copy-paste. Your vision, our expertise. We use only the finest inks and sterilization protocols to ensure your safety and the longevity of your art.
