OUR STORY

Born from Skill,
Built on Craft.

Ahura Ink Studio was founded in 2014 in the heart of Istanbul. Five artists from around the world — each with a strong skill set, a different backstory, and a distinct vision of art — were brought together by the studio's founder to create a place where every corner of the world leaves its mark. With precise attention to standards and trends, blended with the true soul of tattoo art, this is more than just a tattoo shop. This is where you carve your story into permanence — and where it will be remembered forever.
