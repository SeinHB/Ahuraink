OUR SPECIALTIES

Colourful Tattoos

Vibrant, bold colour work that stands out and stays vivid. From watercolour washes to saturated neo-traditional — full-spectrum art on skin.

Full Designs

Complete sleeves, back pieces, and large-scale compositions. Cohesive multi-session works that tell a full visual story across your canvas.

Line Art

OUR STORY

Born From Darkness,
Built on Craft.

Ahura Ink Studio was founded in 2013 in the heart of Istanbul by artists obsessed with the craft of tattooing. We believe a tattoo is not just ink — it is a permanent story, a ritual, a declaration.

Every piece we create is custom-designed. No flash sheets, no copy-paste. Your vision, our expertise. We use only the finest inks and sterilization protocols to ensure your safety and the longevity of your art.
